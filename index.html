Evening

6:00

Sterilise food bowls & Steam food

  • Sterilise the ceramic food bowls and spoon for the 50 minutes duration.
  • Set up the rice cooker: Fill with water, then place the steaming basket and green ceramic plate in.
  • Steam 1-2 pumpkin slices.
  • Using the steam function of the rice cooker, for 40 minutes.

6:45–7:00

Pumpkins done, Steam wet food

  • Be careful when opening the lid of the rice cooker, as the steam is very hot.
  • Remove the pumpkins into a bowl.
  • Take the two leftover half cans of wet food from breakfast out of the fridge (one Kitten, one Adult). Steam them one at a time in the rice cooker, 5 minutes each.
  • Once the sterilisation is completed, open the door of the steriliser and let the food bowls cool a little (cool enough for you to hold and also not to burn the cats as they lick their food off the bowls)
  • Split the steamed pumpkins into 3 servings and scoop them into each food bowl.
  • After steaming the wet food for 5 minutes each, total of 10 minutes, move them from the rice cooker plate into the respective food bowls.

8:00 – bedtime

Free time for the kiddos

To note
Supper feed is at 9pm.

It is encouraged to keep Beans in master bedroom, and Bonnie and Tifa in the cats room BEFORE the automatic feeders dispense their food.

Beans is having adult food while the other 2 kiddos are having kitten food. Please try not to mix, and separate them if they dash off to the opposite rooms.

  • Clear the kiddos' pee and poop from the litter boxes in both the master bedroom and cats room one last time.
